Kitchen Plus Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Kitchen Plus ("we", "our", "the app") helps you track kitchen inventory, scan receipts, and plan meals. This page explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and how it's protected.

Information We Collect

  • Phone number — used to sign you in via one-time SMS code (no password required).
  • Name — used to personalize your account within the app.
  • Kitchen inventory data — items you add, their categories, storage location, and purchase dates.
  • Receipt photos and scanned data — images of receipts you choose to scan, and the store name, items, and totals extracted from them.
  • Shopping list and meal plan data — items and recipes you save for future use.

How We Use This Information

All data collected is used solely to provide the app's core functionality: authenticating your account, storing your kitchen inventory, tracking food freshness, matching recipes to what you have, and managing your shopping list and meal plans. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

Kitchen Plus uses Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions) to store and process your data securely. Receipt scanning uses Google's Gemini AI, called only from our own backend — your data is never sent directly to Gemini from your device, and no third party uses your data to track you across other apps or websites.

Data Security

Your data is private to your account. Server-side security rules ensure that only you can read or write your own inventory, receipts, and shopping list — no other user of the app can access your data.

Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data (inventory, receipts, receipt photos, shopping lists, meal plans) at any time from within the app's account settings.

Children's Privacy

Kitchen Plus is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Contact Us
